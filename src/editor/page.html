SCRATCH SLAYER

Map Editor

New map Starts with a wall all round, the player start P and the exit switch X.

Tool

Right-click paints floor. Hold Shift and click for a straight line. Alt-click picks up the letter under the mouse.

Letter

Or type the letter on the keyboard: M for an imp, 3 for wall 3, 0 for floor.

Columns Rows
Point at a square to see what it is.

Map check

The same checks npm test runs. Point at a problem to see its square on the map.

    Balance

    Ammo ratio: damage all the ammo on the map can do ÷ the health of all its monsters. Bigger is easier. Health: what the health packs give.

    Picture settings

    These live in the map's record in src/levels.py, not in the file. Here they only change the picture.

    Map text

    Exactly what goes in the file: one row per line. Type or paste here and the map follows.
